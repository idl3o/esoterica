# v2 TOPICS — the re-cut

How the 111 v1 films fold into v2. Every v1 number appears exactly once; `python apparatus/scripts/slate_v2.py recut` checks that. The **rulers** column lists the outside sources a gatherer starts from. It is a floor, not a bibliography. **hand = Sam** means the topic needs Sam's approval before it renders: house theses the slate's conclusions rest on, the repository's own method, and the self-portrait.

Kinds: **exposition** and **thesis** get a dossier and a script; **work** carries over unchanged; **sam** waits for Sam's direction. A **light** note marks a wave-5 film that one hand already wrote against the house register. Its v2 pass is the dossier and the checks; its script changes only where they find something.

The tally is 53 topics remade from 85 v1 films, 21 works carried over, and 5 films held for Sam.

## Proposed strand order after the pilot

Norse → House cosmology → Traditions → Contact → Teachers → Fictions → the light pass. The Norse strand goes first because its rulers are the sharpest (the Eddas, cited by stanza) and it folds the most (20 films into 8). House cosmology follows because it is where the truest stance will move the slate's conclusions most.
