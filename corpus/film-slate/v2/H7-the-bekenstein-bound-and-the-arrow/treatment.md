# Treatment — The Bekenstein Bound and the Arrow

## Central image

A sealed metal box on a cord, lowered slowly by a hand winch towards a perfectly black sphere. This is Bekenstein's 1981 gedanken experiment, staged as a laboratory demonstration would have been photographed in his time. It recurs by being shown again, never re-described:

- **The Box on the String:** lowered, the cord taut, the black sphere filling the lower frame.
- **Area and Energy:** the same box beside the sphere, small against its curve, with light from behind bent around the sphere's edge.
- **Thirty Years of Argument:** the box opened on a workbench, its walls, plates and fittings laid out to be counted.
- **The Difference from the Vacuum:** two identical boxes side by side, one with its contents and one empty, under the same light.
- **Where the Arrow Is Not:** the box at rest on the bench while, beyond it, breath fades from a cold window.
- **Where the Observer Is:** the box seen from a moving vehicle's window, blurred and then sharp.
- **What Is Declined:** the cord cut and coiled, the box closed on the bench.

## Image register

Mid-twentieth-century physics photography, in black and white and in muted colour: brass, steel, cord, enamel, frosted glass, bench lamps. The places are real and plain: the Hebrew University campus in Jerusalem, the lakeside at Bariloche below the Andes, the Institute for Advanced Study at Princeton under snow, a lecture room with an empty blackboard wiped clean. The light is that of a working room.

## What the sources show, by movement

- **The Box on the String:** the box, the cord, the winch, the black sphere; a box of hot gas; a ruler held against the box's widest side.
- **Area and Energy:** an optical hologram plate on a bench, lit by a laser; lasers mounted on a flat wall flashing at once; four bundles of light rays leaving a small disc, two forward and two back; light grazing the Sun.
- **Thirty Years of Argument:** a great many parallel conducting plates; a coaxial cable cut to show its layers; a single photon detector; a cavity with heavy walls.
- **The Difference from the Vacuum:** a half-space bounded by a flat wall, a small object far from it; the curved paths of steadily accelerating trajectories, drawn as wire hyperbolae; a sphere with the curves of a flow inside it.
- **Where the Arrow Is Not:** a gas released at the centre of an empty chamber and spreading; a film of the process played backwards; an evaporating black hole with matter still falling in; breath on a cold pane fading.
- **Where the Observer Is:** a de Sitter static patch drawn as a sphere with one worldline through its centre; several clocks of different makes on one table, each showing a different time; a train window at speed.
- **What Is Declined:** lungs, cortex and soil photographed as what they are, in a medical atlas and a soil pit; the box closed.

## Never shown

- Galaxies, nebulae or star fields as decoration.
- A glowing grid, a hologram fragment containing the whole scene, or a surface "facing itself".
- The fogged pane presented as the bound. It appears once, as ordinary evaporation.
- Eyes, lone figures under the sky, doors ajar, or dawn light.
- Equations, letters or any written text.
