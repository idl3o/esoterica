# Treatment — The Death of Baldr

## Central image

A sprig of mistletoe, real and botanical: its forked green stems, paired leaves and translucent white berries, growing high on the branch of a winter tree. It is shown again from a new place in each movement, never re-described:

- **The Dreams:** on the branch against a grey sky.
- **The Oath and the Sprout:** a single shoot, westward of a hall, too slight to notice.
- **The Shot:** in a hand, stripped to a wand.
- **The Ship and the Ride:** a twig blackening in the ash of a pyre.
- **The Weeping and Thokk:** frost melting off its berries.
- **Vali:** trodden into mud.
- **The Binding and the Bowl:** absent. The bowl takes its place.
- **Saxo's Balder:** a sword in a frozen scabbard instead.
- **After the Burning:** a new shoot on a young tree in a green field.

## Image register

Icelandic and Scandinavian landscape and museum photography: basalt shores, lava fields under snow, birch, turf houses, rain on a fjord. The objects are those of the Viking Age as they survive in museums: ship timbers and rivets, carved panels, iron, gold rings, the Gosforth Cross in its churchyard in Cumbria, and medieval Icelandic manuscript leaves photographed as objects, closed or at a distance, with no legible text. The gods are never shown with faces. They are shown by what they hold, wear and ride.

## What the sources show, by movement

- **Where the Story Is Kept:** a manuscript codex on a reading desk; the ruins of Snorri's farm at Reykholt; a carved house panel; a Danish chronicle.
- **The Dreams:** an eight-legged horse's tracks in snow; a hound with blood on its breast; a burial mound with an eastern door; snow, rain and dew on a grave; benches set with rings; a shield laid over a mead vat.
- **The Oath and the Sprout:** fire, water, iron, stones, earth, trees, beasts, birds and serpents, each in turn; a ring of people throwing stones and spears at one unharmed figure, seen from behind; a hall in a fen.
- **The Shot:** a ring of people with one figure standing outside it; a wand of green wood; a figure fallen on the ground.
- **The Ship and the Ride:** a great ship on log rollers at a shore; a wolf with a viper bridle; sparks from the rollers; a hammer raised over a pyre; a gold ring; a horse in full trappings; a boar-drawn chariot; cats; a bridge thatched with gold over a dark river; a gate leapt by a horse; a high seat in a hall.
- **The Weeping and Thokk:** stones and metal sweating as frost melts in sun; a cave mouth in a lava field.
- **Vali:** an infant's unwashed hands; uncombed hair; a pyre lit at night.
- **The Binding and the Bowl:** three flat stones on edge, each drilled through; iron bonds; a serpent above a bound figure's face; a woman holding a wooden basin under dripping venom, then turning to pour it out; the earth shaking; the Gosforth Cross's carved panel.
- **Saxo's Balder:** a woman bathing seen from far off in reeds; a reindeer sledge over frozen ridges; a sword bound in its scabbard; a war club cut through at the haft; snakes dripping venom into a bowl of food; a litter carried into battle; a barrow.
- **After the Burning:** green earth rising from the sea; waterfalls; an eagle taking fish below cliffs; golden gaming pieces lying in grass; a hall roofed with gold; a hall woven of serpents' backs, its doors facing north.

## Never shown

- Baldr glowing, haloed, or as a figure of light.
- A ship sailing out to sea on fire.
- A hand guiding another hand.
- Christian iconography placed beside Baldr: crosses, crucifixion, haloes. The Gosforth Cross appears only as the Cumbrian monument it is.
- Eyes; lone figures under dawn skies; doors ajar.
- Runes, letters or any legible text.
