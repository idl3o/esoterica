# Treatment — The Witness

## Central image

A railway platform in northern India, photographed as it is: the station master's cabin, the signal lever, the platform edge. It recurs by being shown again from a new distance or hour, never re-described:

- **The Station Master:** a crowded express at the platform, then pulling out, the platform still there.
- **The Light When the Lights Go Out:** the same platform at dusk, then at night under one lamp.
- **The Fourth:** a goods train passing, smoke and one or two figures.
- **The Lamp That Needs No Lamp:** the station's oil lamp on its hook.
- **The Other Answers:** fires by the tracks, each burning a different fuel.
- **The Fridge Door:** the platform lights switching on as a train arrives.
- **A Model of Being Awake:** the night signal box, its instruments lit.
- **What Is Declined:** the empty platform in daylight.

## Image register

Documentary photography of South Asia, past and present: Indian Railways platforms and signal cabins; a Himalayan monastery at Gangotri in snow; Sringeri on the Tungabhadra; manuscript bundles of palm leaf, photographed closed or at a distance. For the outside answers, ordinary laboratory and sleep-clinic photography: an EEG cap, a sleep-lab bed, a meditator in a scanner, all shown plainly and without glow.

## What the sources show, by movement

- **The Station Master:** the Rajdhani Express with its crowd; a goods train with smoke; the empty platform; the station master's flag.
- **The Light When the Lights Go Out:** the sun setting, then the moon, then a fire burning down, then a voice in the dark; a person in darkness unable to see their own hands; a sleeper; two students looking at themselves in a pan of water, then dressed in fine clothes and looking again.
- **The Fourth:** a copper kārṣāpaṇa coin, punch-marked, divided into four quarters; students asleep over lecture notes.
- **The Lamp That Needs No Lamp:** mother-of-pearl beside silver; the moon seen double through a lens; the blue sky over Kanpur; one oil lamp beside an unlit second lamp; a jewel coloured by sunlight through red flowers; a rope in dim light; an eye; a red-hot iron ball in a forge; the sun reflected in the water of a clay pot.
- **The Other Answers:** a knife on a table; six fires burning wood, wood-chips, grass, cow-dung, chaff and rubbish; a cow and a water-jar.
- **The Fridge Door:** a refrigerator door opened again and again, the light on each time; a student pausing mid-step.
- **A Model of Being Awake:** an EEG trace of a waking brain; a sleeper waking in a sleep lab; a meditator seated, eyes closed.
- **What Is Declined:** the empty platform; a closed manuscript bundle.

## Never shown

- The threshold of sleep presented as the witness.
- A light, flame or glowing figure standing for consciousness itself. The sources use lamps as analogies, never as the thing.
- One light shared across many faces, or many faces merging into one.
- Eyes looking out of the frame, lone figures under dawn skies, doors ajar.
- Sanskrit, Devanagari or any legible text.
