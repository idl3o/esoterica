# The Vignette Collection

## Head-canon of the bridges — the unwritten mornings

Where a [fiction bridge](../fiction-bridges/) *extracts* the consciousness technology already in a canon, a **head-canon vignette** *extends* the canon into a scene the story never wrote — usually an ordinary moment, off the plot's timeline — and reads the insight off what the scene is **forced to invent**. The pun is the method: *canonisation* is how a saint is made, and each vignette puts a character's stance on trial — does the technology stay *livable* once the story stops protecting it? Built with **[`/head-canon`](../../.claude/commands/head-canon.md)**.

Every vignette carries a provenance footer:

> *Head-canon of [[source-bridge]] — <the displacement used> · <the register found>.*

---

## Why this is organized by register

The collection is sorted by **register** (its aesthetic key) on purpose. When head-canon was run as a *delegated wave* — many agents, one shared command — it clumped: every vignette collapsed onto whatever tone the command named, regardless of source (first a uniform melancholy, then, after a fix, a uniform warmth). The finding, now a standing pattern ([[shared-target-monoculture]]): **tone cannot be produced by instructing each writer; it must be assigned per-source by one mind that sees the whole set.**

So this document *is* that one mind's map. Sorting by register makes the **coverage visible** — which keys are filled, which are thin, which are empty — and turns "avoid the monoculture" into a concrete task: **generate into the gaps.** Adding vignettes is a tone-negative-space exercise. Don't ask for "another good vignette"; assign the register the collection lacks.

## The collection

### ◆ Beauty — *mono no aware*
- **[Still](invincible-still.md)** — *[[invincible-the-inheritance-offered-in-love|Invincible]]* · deep-time (Mark & Eve, ~4,000 years on). The un-remade mug: the matter-goddess forbidden by love to make a perfect copy of the one worn, mortal thing. Transience and preciousness as a single fact; the crack held open to the light. *The origin piece.*

### ◆ Cold institutional horror
- **[The Fitting](the-emperor-on-the-golden-throne.md)** — *Warhammer 40,000* (no bridge; origin fragment) · deep-time (the Golden Throne). A hereditary servant polishes a fitting that needs no polishing on a god who cannot be allowed to rest. The exact inverse of *Still*: immortality imposed, preserved-perfect against its will — deep-time as damnation. *The falsification-test piece — proof the method reads the world, not the mood.*

### ◆ Warm comedy — domestic, with an edge underneath
*(The pilot batch. All six landed here when the wave over-corrected — kept together honestly as the record of that clump. The strongest keep grief or discomfort under the warmth; "Gary" is kept as the purely-cozy outlier that documents the over-correction.)*
- **[The Step That Goes Down](dragonball-the-training-never-ends.md)** — *[[dragonball-the-training-never-ends|Dragon Ball]]* · a broken-gravity-chamber afternoon. Vegeta, the being whose whole technology is *become more*, must get a crying baby to sleep — and discovers the staircase has a step that goes *down*. "Barely there took everything."
- **[The Stew at the Fog Gate](elden-ring-the-shattering-and-the-stone.md)** — *[[elden-ring-the-shattering-and-the-stone|Elden Ring]]* · a merchant's cook-fire beside a fog gate. A mortal with "no chime up the road" gives a deathless Tarnished a free bowl and a winning tip. The once-only stew as the counter-value to cheap respawn. *(Grief under the comedy.)*
- **[Cleaning Duty](sailor-moon-the-crybaby-and-the-cosmos.md)** — *[[sailor-moon-the-crybaby-and-the-cosmos|Sailor Moon]]* · an ordinary school-cleaning afternoon. Usagi's tears heal no one — and land as *trespass* on a classmate who chose numbness on purpose. Compassion as imposition. *(Real discomfort under the warmth.)*
- **[The Warranty](loki-the-lie-that-tells-the-truth.md)** — *[[loki-the-lie-that-tells-the-truth|Loki]]* · a repair-day in the wronged goddess's kitchen. Dwarf-gold hair that *cannot forget*; the agent-of-forgetting forges the one thing that can't let go, and fixes it with a lie that comes out true.
- **[The Back Door of the Blue Comet](star-trek-the-interior-frontier.md)** — *[[star-trek-the-interior-frontier|Star Trek]]* · a maintenance shift, told from *inside* the simulation. A holo-cook props his own back door open to see the edge of his world, then negotiates a window. Finitude as curiosity, not tragedy.
- **[Gary](moonknight-khonshu-consciousness-integration.md)** — *[[moonknight-khonshu-consciousness-integration|Moon Knight]]* · a new-moon Tuesday, one stray dog. Three alters keep a body — and now a dog — alive by chore-rota and a shared notebook. Integration as logistics, not merger. *(The over-corrected outlier: a trauma-horror source rendered fully cozy — kept as evidence.)*

## Registers still unwritten *(the gaps — generate here)*
The map is lopsided: two singletons and a warm-comic pile. The keys the collection has *not* yet honestly reached — each a standing invitation for a register-assigned run:

- **Tragic / elegiac without consolation** — a loss the scene refuses to redeem.
- **Terror / dread** — not undecidable-melancholy (the v1 trap), but real fear.
- **Farce / absurdist** — comedy that stays comedy, no tender undertow.
- **Sacred / numinous** — awe held straight, not ironized.
- **Clinical / procedural cold** — the flat institutional voice, earned.
- **Erotic / tender-embodied** — intimacy as the register.
- **Rage / satire** — the edged, unforgiving key.

## How to add a vignette
1. Pick a **[bridge](../fiction-bridges/)** with continuable life (character/narrative, not a concept-bridge).
2. **Assign the register** from the gaps above — do *not* let the writer default to the collection's house tone. This is the one instruction the delegated wave couldn't self-supply.
3. Run **[`/head-canon`](../../.claude/commands/head-canon.md)** (hand-tool: one bridge at a time, one mind matching register to source).
4. File it under `vignettes/<slug>.md` with its provenance footer, and add its entry here under the right register.
5. **Watch the map.** If a register starts to pile up, you're clumping again — move to a gap.
