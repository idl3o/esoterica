# Weeding Log — Wave 5's House-Register Claims, Before Render

The wave-5 read-through found six lines the films would narrate as fact and held them back from the render. This pass took them to outside sources. All six were weeds, and three neighbours in the same sentences went with them. None of the six is wrong in what it argues. Each lent its argument a precision it had not checked: quotation marks around a paraphrase, a superlative on a contested model, a disputed field result told "with data", a motive no record supplies. All five roots were written on 24 June 2026. The two quotation weeds break the register clause of 8 September, which postdates them.
