The five documents grown on 4–5 September 2026 were written *as* the corpus's audit of itself: two of them price the founding convergence, one prices the OS, one prices the residual, one prices the library's own cohesion. This pass asks the question those documents could not ask from inside: are the external facts they lean on true? The answer is that the philosophical apparatus survives almost untouched and the worked examples do not. The pattern is the same in all five: the method is sound and the material it was run on was never checked. That is the shape the `/weed` skill predicts for a convergence-engine — the frame is where the attention goes, and the facts ride in underneath it.
