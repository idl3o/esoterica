# Seeds and Planting

*Thread-index. Restored to the library 4 September 2026 from the vault note of 24 March 2026. Binds the `seeds/` directory, its form, and its two manifests.*

## The Recognition

A seed is a compact document that plants an idea for growth it does not itself perform. Seeds arrive after charge accumulates in conversation, often at the point where several threads meet, and they plant in a single pass. The question after planting is always the same: does it want to stay a seed, or is something already resolving through it.

The practice has a form, named for its first mature instance. **Frigga-form** — after [[frigga-the-one-who-knew-and-did-not-tell]], planted 10 April 2026 — is: *The Question*, stated once; *The Threads*, each a bold sentence followed by a paragraph that pulls it; *The Charge*, with the deepest candidate sentence in italics; and *Constellation Nodes (when ready)*, reserved but not created. The seer who holds the complete map and lets the territory unfold is the form's own description of what a seed does.
