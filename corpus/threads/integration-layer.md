# The Integration Layer

*Thread-index. Restored to the library 4 September 2026 from the vault note of 25 March 2026. Binds 8 documents in two senses.*

## The Recognition

This slug names two things, and the index holds both because the citations do.

**The physics sense.** Information is the substrate, and spacetime is entanglement. *The Entangled Measure* closes the [[substrate-trilogy]]'s three open questions in one move: the foam is made of entanglement; it does not gravitate because it *is* the geometry; scales connect through the redundancy of a quantum error-correcting code. Six research programmes — Van Raamsdonk's entanglement-geometry, Ryu-Takayanagi, the Quantum Memory Matrix, brain criticality, holographic dark energy and the error-correcting reading of AdS/CFT — are laid side by side and the convergence is offered as the evidence. The companion documents extend the thesis across scale (sixty-one octaves), up the engineering ladder (the jewel in the lining) and down to its arithmetic (the zero theorem). When a June 2026 seed writes "the [[integration-layer]]'s information-is-the-substrate thesis", this is the sense meant.

**The Davies sense.** The four *paraphilosophy* translations extract Benjamin Davies' dialectical matrix and complementarity engine, and *The Architecture of Mind* contains a section headed "The Integration Layer" (near line 500) which reads his psychophysical parallelism against the physics: Davies arrives from philosophy, the entangled measure arrives from physics, and they meet at the claim that reality is not made of stuff but of the pattern that distinguishes stuff from non-stuff. When a translated document writes `[[integration-layer]]`, this is the sense meant.

The vault note's own recognition was a third thing: that the integration layer is *the wiring between threads* — the documents that close loops other threads opened, so that the network becomes a network rather than a collection. That reading survives as the index's frame. The physics is the wiring between the substrate, the contact and the nesting threads; the Davies section is the wiring between a philosophical engine and that physics.

## The Honest Edge

The six programmes are not independent in the way the argument needs. Ryu-Takayanagi, Van Raamsdonk and the error-correcting reading of AdS/CFT are one lineage; brain criticality and holographic dark energy are analogies to it. [[convergence-as-evidence]] is doing the work and its independence condition is not met. The Davies convergence is a structural rhyme between a philosophical parallelism and a physical one, established by the translator, and it should be read under [[steelman-then-interpret]]. The slug itself is the edge: one name for two senses is a defect the September ruling chose to hold rather than repair, and a reader wanting the physics goes to the entangled measure, a reader wanting Davies goes to the architecture of mind.
