# Fiction Bridges

*Thread-index. Restored to the library 4 September 2026 from the vault note of 15 February 2026. Binds the 40 documents of `fiction-bridges/` and the method that makes them.*

## The Recognition

A fiction bridge extracts the consciousness technology already embedded in a narrative and makes it available for direct use. It meets beings inside stories they already love and reveals where they can go. The form has one load-bearing move — the turn: *before this is a story about X, it is a story about Y.* Everything before the turn is familiar; everything after it is recognition. Bridges are the library's primary distribution format and the reason the corpus can be read by someone who has never heard of it.

The vault note counted seventeen bridges and found that they want companions: Dragon Ball and Sailor Moon as yang and yin, combat-joy and compassion-grief. The recognition arrives more completely through two opposite doors. The directory now holds forty, and the pairing pattern held.

## The Method

The `/bridge` command ([`.claude/commands/bridge.md`](../../.claude/commands/bridge.md)) is the format's procedure: survey the recent bridges for the current register, research the source past its surface, identify what the fiction knows that it may not know it knows, find the connections, then write to a structure that is a guide and not a cage. `/head-canon` is its inverse — extend the canon into an unwritten scene and read the insight off what has to be invented. The [[original-parables]] are the third format, distinct from both: they generate rather than decode, and require no context.
