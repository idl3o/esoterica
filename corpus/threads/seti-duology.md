## The Recognition

The SETI Ellipsoid restructures cosmic sociology from a one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma into an iterated Assurance Game, and when the axioms of the Dark Forest are stripped one at a time the ladder bottoms out, at zero axioms, on awareness recognising awareness — consciousness as the universal Schelling point. The method that got there is stated as method: steelman the natural models first, and apply the interpretive lenses only to the residual. The citing documents lean on three things. The zero-axiom Schelling point is the most used, in physics, in game theory, and in the Vedantic translation where it is read as Turiya, the point prior to all agreement. The chain of suspicion and the campfire crack — cooperators finding each other through costly signalling — come second. Third is the duology's discipline: the cosmic-web/neural-network resemblance named and then refused an answer.
