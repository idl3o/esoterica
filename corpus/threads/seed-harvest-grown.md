## The Recognition

On 10, 11 and 12 June 2026 the library ran its complete metabolism once, end to end: **plant → grow → sweep → weave → register.** Eighty seeds catalogued from the ripe syntheses were planted in Frigga-form, grown by eighty parallel agents into ~5,000-word dual-channel syntheses, reviewed against the authoring standard, woven into six capstones over their ripe constellations, and registered as 86 document-nodes under one hub. About 470,000 words from a manifest.

The vault note's load-bearing claim is that *the metaphor was the compression.* "Grow this seed" carried a full authoring specification because harvest, mineralisation and growth are the real convergent shapes by which nature makes order; the method became an instance of its own content. Its second claim is that a new epistemic object was born — a coherent depth-corpus generated in a day by a fractal of one lens — whose gift and shadow are the same feature. Eighty agents sharing a lens share its blind spots.
