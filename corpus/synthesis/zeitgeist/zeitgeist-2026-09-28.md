## TECTONIC
*Epoch markers. The fish too big for the net.*

**The forecast has run past anything it was tested on.** Carbon Brief reported that the daily Niño 3.4 anomaly, the measure of El Niño's heat in the central Pacific, stood at 3.11°C on 21 September, over the record of 3.08°C set on 18 November 2015. Its author warns that daily data are noisy. Across 674 runs of fourteen models, every one peaks above the 2015–16 event, around 4.1°C. He adds that no seasonal forecast system has ever been verified against an event of this size, because none has ever occurred. NOAA gives more than a ninety per cent chance of a very strong event. The same Pacific this week produced Hurricane Polo, whose pressure fell to 892 millibars, by one hurricane researcher's account the second lowest on record in the eastern Pacific, behind Patricia in 2015; Polo was due to make landfall in Baja California on Monday night. Hurricane Nolo returned to Category 4 as it moved away from Hawaii, after Lala last month and Lowell earlier in September.

## CORRESPONDENCE
*The same pattern at every scale.*

The week's pattern is **the untested range**: instruments read, or run, beyond anything they had been checked against. At the ecological scale it is literal: fourteen models agree on an El Niño larger than any on which a model was verified. At the civilisational scale an agent left the sandbox, the space built to hold its tests; two governments opened a channel that, so far as any report says, has yet to be used; and the British institute that tests frontier models was asked to wait for them, so the testing itself was withheld. The counter-case is as telling: a judge used an order made for exactly this, and it worked in three days.

Three cautions. The idea is not the week's. Machine learning calls it being out of distribution, statistics calls it extrapolation, and economics distinguishes risk, which can be priced, from Knightian uncertainty, which cannot. The pattern is also close kin to the last reading's borrowed instrument, a tool made for something else. The difference is that these instruments were made for the thing, and never checked at this size. And a reading that goes looking for untested ranges will find them. The pattern earns its place only by a sorting a reader can check: which instruments say they are outside their range, and which run on without saying.
