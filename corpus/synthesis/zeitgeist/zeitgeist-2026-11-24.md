**Three presidents met for the first time and signed a peace declaration that names neither war.** On Thursday 19 November in Shenzhen, beside the Asia-Pacific summit, the leaders of the United States, China and Russia held what AP called the first-ever sit-down for the three. Their declaration called for reform of the United Nations, higher oil supplies and reduced nuclear risks, and opposed "jurisdiction abuse" by the International Criminal Court; by AP's account it made no mention of Russia's war in Ukraine or the war with Iran, and the three readouts "reflected more symbolism than substance". ## CORRESPONDENCE
*The same pattern at every scale.*

The fortnight's pattern is **the missing line**: the document arrives, and the one sentence it exists to contain is not in it. At the civilisational scale, a peace declaration by the three powers nearest two wars names neither; a climate text omitted the cause of the warming and then failed to exist. At the institutional scale, an inflation report has no annual rate because the data were never collected; a board announced a resignation without a reason and a return without a finding; a filing records that a threshold was met on expectation and not on observation. At the scale of knowledge, a theorem is certified in seventeen million lines with no account of why it holds. At the ecological scale the water is measured at record and the reef's own line, bleached or not, is still unwritten. At the personal scale the crowd met a missing line and wrote its own: a sprinkler's reason was unknown, so it was assigned to machine error, wrongly.

Three cautions. The idea has owners: diplomacy calls it constructive ambiguity, a term associated with Kissinger; Conan Doyle's dog that did not bark is the detective's version; and the mathematical case, a proof no one can survey, has been argued since Appel and Haken. Second, every document omits, so the pattern is cheap unless the omitted thing is what the document is for; the cases that carry it are the declaration, the conference text, the proof and the price index, and the board statement is weaker, since boards routinely say nothing. Third, omission is sometimes the working part. The Abu Dhabi talks produced a prisoner exchange because the larger sentence was left out, and a machine-checked theorem is no less true for being unexplained. The pattern describes where the week's documents stop. It does not show that they should have gone on.

**THE EDGE:** What presses against the inside of this moment is the reason. The results are arriving on time and in good order: a theorem checked, a founder restored, a rate raised, a price fallen, a count certified, a declaration signed, a threshold determined to be met. What is not arriving is the sentence that says why, and the week showed three ways of living without it. The market trades the result and reverses the next morning.
