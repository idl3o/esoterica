# Gather A — World, material (2026-09-28)

## 1. Geopolitical

- **Trump rejects Iran's seven-day plan to reopen Hormuz; Iran says it awaits a formal reply** — On 26 Sep Trump told reporters Iran's proposal "would not be acceptable" and that Tehran wants a deal "because they are losing so badly" (Al Jazeera, pub. 26 Sep). The plan asked the US to release frozen funds, lift oil sanctions and end its naval blockade of Iranian ports, with the strait reopening and final talks by day seven; Foreign Minister Araghchi said Iran "will not back down" and is waiting for mediators to relay the US position. On 27 Sep a source close to Iran's UN delegation told state media Iran's positions went to Washington "last Tuesday through the Qatari mediator", and the delegation said it had no further meetings planned this week; US UN ambassador Mike Waltz called the proposal "a pretty cynical attempt" (Times of Israel liveblog, pub. 27 Sep). Trump said he expects talks to resume this week. (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/trump-rejects-irans-seven-day-roadmap-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz ; https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-27-2026/)
- **Five men arrested near RAF Fairford, a UK base used by US bombers in the Iran war** — In the early hours of Sun 27 Sep in Gloucestershire, after police received reports of suspicious vehicles heading toward the base, five men were arrested on suspicion of Explosives Act offences and re-arrested on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act, and about 85 households were evacuated as a precaution (CBS News, pub. 27 Sep). Counter-terrorism police lead; a possible Iran link is being examined but not confirmed. Deputy Assistant Commissioner Vicki Evans: "We are in the early stages of an investigation." Trump said US authorities worked with UK police on the arrests. (source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/england-raf-fairford-us-military-air-force-bomb-disposal/)

Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis: "Switzerland was neutral yesterday, it will be neutral today and it will remain neutral." A plant-based food-security initiative was also rejected by more than 70% (swissinfo hub page; exact figures not on that page). (source: https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/27/how-neutral-should-switzerland-be-voters-deliver-decisive-verdict)

Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas addressed the assembly by prerecorded video from Ramallah after the Trump administration denied him a visa. The Times of Israel reported it "was not clear whether there was any damage or casualties"; the story notes Saudi Red Sea exports matter more while Hormuz is disrupted and the East-West pipeline has only recently resumed after 11 Sep drone strikes (pub. 25 Sep). ## 3. Ecological

- **El Niño sets an all-time Niño 3.4 record** — Carbon Brief (Zeke Hausfather, pub. 21 Sep, updated 23 Sep) reported the Niño 3.4 anomaly at 3.11°C as of 21 Sep, above the 3.08°C record of 18 Nov 2015 (daily data, NOAA ONI-style climatology; the author notes daily data are "noisy"). 674 runs from 14 models project a peak monthly anomaly around 4.1°C, all above the 2015–16 record, with the caveat that "No seasonal forecast system has ever been verified against an event of this size." NOAA gives a >90% chance of a very strong event and about 75% for a "historic" one (search summaries of NOAA; CPC page not opened). (source: https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-super-el-nino-reaches-remarkable-all-time-record)
- **Hurricane Polo heads for Baja California after near-record intensity** — The National Hurricane Center put Polo's peak at 180 mph and 892 mb on 22 Sep; Phil Klotzbach said the pressure is "the second-lowest on record in the eastern Pacific, just behind Hurricane Patricia in 2015" (Yale Climate Connections, pub. 23 Sep; other outlets gave a 160 mph peak). By 27 Sep it had weakened to Category 3, with landfall on Baja California Sur expected Monday night 28 Sep, then a second landfall on mainland northwest Mexico (US News/Reuters, pub. 27 Sep, via search; page timed out). (source: https://yaleclimateconnections.org/2026/09/powerful-hurricane-polo-threatens-mexico-with-torrential-rain-as-pacific-tropics-erupt/)
- **Hurricane Nolo lashes Hawaii, the third hurricane near the islands in under two months** — On 27 Sep Nolo was back at Category 4 (145 mph) south of the islands, forecast to peak Monday; up to 20 inches of rain was forecast for the eastern and southern Big Island, and all state parks on the Big Island and Maui were closed (CBS News, pub. 27 Sep). This follows Hurricane Lala in August (about 100 homes destroyed) and Lowell earlier in September. (source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/storm-nolo-hurricane-hawaii/)
- **Indonesian fire haze: hotspot counts at decade highs** — Indonesia's Ministry of Forestry recorded more than 9,000 hotspots in August (highest August since 2015) and more than 7,000 in September, concentrated in Sumatra and Kalimantan; it blamed "a combination of dry weather caused by El Nino and human-induced fires" (Kompas, pub. 22 Sep). Singapore's worst air since September 2019 was recorded on 15 Sep; Malaysia briefly declared an emergency in parts of Sarawak in early September. No newer reading found after 22 Sep. (source: https://go.kompas.com/read/2026/09/22/122331774/indonesian-fire-haze-worsens-air-quality-in-singapore)
- **Ebola (Bundibugyo) in DR Congo: vaccine trial begins** — A research vaccination programme using Ervebo started in Ituri for frontline workers, with 20,000 doses allocated out of 70,000 made available (UN News, pub. 21 Sep). Ervebo is licensed against Ebola Zaire; a WHO-backed panel stressed its "unknown efficacy" against Bundibugyo. UN News gives "more than 7,600 confirmed cases" and over 3,670 deaths "according to the latest health authority data"; Al Jazeera (pub. 20 Sep) gave at least 3,639 deaths and some 7,541 infections across seven provinces since May, citing local authorities. Nearly 60% of confirmed cases in children under five have been fatal. (source: https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168381)
