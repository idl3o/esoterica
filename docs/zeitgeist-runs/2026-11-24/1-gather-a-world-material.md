- **COP31 (continuity): ends in Antalya without a cover decision** — Closed Sun 22 Nov 2026 at 7:33pm local time, nearly 50 hours late; Carbon Brief published about 23 Nov (no explicit date on page), Democracy Now 23 Nov.
